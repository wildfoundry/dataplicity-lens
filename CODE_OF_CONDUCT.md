# Code of conduct

We want Dataplicity Lens to be a technically serious and welcoming project.

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone is
representing the project or WildFoundry.

Be direct without being demeaning. Critique code and decisions, not people. Assume good faith, explain
trade-offs, and make room for contributors with different levels of Linux and Rust experience.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and sustained disruptive behaviour are not accepted.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and communications that violate these rules and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the
maintainers through the repository's private vulnerability-reporting channel. Include links or
screenshots where possible. Reports are handled confidentially and retaliation is not accepted.

Maintainers will consider context, impact and any pattern of behaviour. Responses may range from a
private correction or content removal to a temporary or permanent ban. A maintainer named in a report
will not make the final decision on that report.
