Services

Check services and their processes.

Inspect systemd units on Linux and launchd services on macOS, including related process counts, failures and restarts.

On this pageUsePlatformsUnavailable data

Use

shell 
lens-services --plain
lens-services --service nginx
lens-services --json
lens-services --action restart --target nginx.service --dry-run

In the interactive Linux view, select a unit and press a to choose an action, review the exact target and confirm it.

Linux and macOS service managers

Lens reads systemd unit state on Linux and launchd service state on macOS. Failed services and probable restart loops are shown with the related service and process.

On systemd Linux, start, stop, restart, enable or disable one exact unit from the interactive action menu or CLI. Lens uses the invoking account and verifies the resulting service state. launchd actions are not enabled until they can use an equally precise target and verification contract.

If service data is unavailable

If the service manager is absent or inaccessible, the command reports a collection warning and returns the data it could read.
