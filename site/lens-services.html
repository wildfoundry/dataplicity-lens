Services

Service state with process context.

Inspect systemd units on Linux and launchd services on macOS, with related process counts and evidence-backed findings.

On this pageUsePlatformsSafety

Use

shell 
lens-services --plain
lens-services --service nginx
lens-services --demo --json

Native service managers

Lens reads systemd unit state on Linux and launchd service state on macOS. Failed services and probable restart loops become shared health findings linked to their service and process context.

Safe when data is limited

The command is read-only. If the service manager is absent or inaccessible, output remains valid and includes a collection warning.
