Suite / lens-logs
Local logs

Recent messages, connected to context.

Read local logs, infer severity and service context, and fold adjacent repeats without hiding their count.

On this pageUseSourcesPermissions

Use

shell 
lens-logs --since "1 hour ago"
lens-logs --service ssh --severity error
lens-logs --log-file /var/log/my-app.log
lens-logs --demo --json

Native sources

Lens reads the systemd journal on Linux and the unified log on macOS. It can also inspect an explicitly selected local log file.

Your permissions stay intact

Log access follows the invoking user's permissions. Missing access is reported as unavailable data; Lens does not request elevation.
