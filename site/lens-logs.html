Lens suite

lens-logs

Reads recent local journal messages, infers service and severity context, and folds adjacent repeated messages without hiding the repeat count.

lens-logs --since "1 hour ago"
lens-logs --service ssh --process sshd --severity error
lens-logs --log-file /var/log/my-app.log
lens-logs --demo --json

Journal access follows the invoking user's permissions. Missing access is reported as unavailable data.
