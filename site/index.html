THE TOOLKIT

One machine.
A whole lot less mystery.

You already have the system tools.
Lens brings their information together, with shared context and a consistent way to explore it.

lens↗

Start with the big picture.

A host cockpit for an overview of your machine. Open a specialist when something needs a closer look.

YOUR STARTING POINT
lens-top↗

Find what’s busy.

Live CPU, memory and I/O. Search, filter, group and inspect the processes behind the numbers.
lens-services↗

See what’s running. Or isn’t.

systemd and launchd service state, restart loops and related processes.
lens-containers↗

Look inside the workload.

Docker, Podman, nerdctl and local LXD inventory, with live performance charts and guarded actions.
lens-logs↗

Read the evidence.

Recent journal, macOS unified log and local file messages. Narrow by service, process or severity.
lens-health↗

Get a useful next step.

Health findings with evidence and suggested follow-ups. Unavailable data stays visible.
