Storage

Check filesystem and mount usage.

See devices, filesystems, mounts, byte and inode use, plus deleted-but-open files where the platform makes them available.

On this pageUseWarningsCurrent behaviour

Use

shell 
lens-disk --plain
lens-disk --filter /var
lens-disk --demo --json

Storage warnings

Usage warnings identify the affected mount and filesystem. Fields that the current platform cannot provide are marked as unavailable.

Current behaviour

lens-disk reports storage state. This release does not remove files, unmount volumes or change storage configuration.
